# SmartSpend - Expense & EMI Tracker 💳

> **Take control of your money, manage loan EMIs, and build your savings with confidence.**

SmartSpend is an all-in-one personal finance app designed to help you track daily expenses, control monthly budgets, manage loans and EMIs, and achieve your savings goals — all with a clean, calm, and delightful mobile experience.

---

## 📱 About the App

Managing money shouldn't feel like a chore. SmartSpend eliminates the stress of budgeting by giving you a clear, real-time snapshot of where your money goes. Whether you want to curb impulse spending, track multiple loan installments, or save for your next dream vacation, SmartSpend keeps you financially disciplined and stress-free.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 💸 Fast & Effortless Expense Tracking
- Log expenses and income in seconds with payment methods (UPI, Cards, Net Banking, Cash).
- Attach photo receipts to keep proof of purchase organized.
- Categorize transactions with customizable icons and pastel color tags.
- Search and filter transaction history by date, category, or merchant.

### 🏦 Comprehensive EMI & Loan Manager
- Track all your loans in one place — Home Loan, Car Loan, Personal Loan, or Gadget EMIs.
- Automated financial calculations: View monthly EMI, principal component, total interest, and remaining tenure.
- **Duplicate-Free Repayment**: Record installment payments with automatic balance deductions and history tracking.
- Due date reminders so you never incur late payment penalties.

### 🎯 Smart Monthly Budgets
- Set spending caps for individual categories (Food, Shopping, Travel, Entertainment) or an overall monthly limit.
- Real-time progress bars notify you when you reach 80% and when you exceed your budget.

### 🏆 Savings Targets & Goals
- Create custom savings targets (Emergency Fund, New Laptop, Holiday Trip).
- Deposit or withdraw funds anytime with visual percentage progress rings.

### 🔄 Recurring Bills & Subscriptions
- Track recurring monthly subscriptions (Netflix, WiFi, Rent, Gym).
- Automatic upcoming due-date alerts and one-tap **"Mark Paid & Advance"**.

### 📊 Visual Analytics & Spending Insights
- **Category Donut Breakdown**: Understand which categories eat the most of your budget.
- **Daily Spending Trend Line**: Identify high-spending days and stay ahead.
- Period filters: View insights by *This Week*, *This Month*, *Last Month*, or *This Year*.

### 📅 Financial Calendar
- A calendar view showing daily expenses, paydays, EMI due dates, and upcoming bills.

### 📄 Export & Statements
- **CSV Export**: One-tap download of all transactions and loan logs compatible with Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets.
- **Printable Statements**: Formatted monthly statements ready to print or save as PDF.
